In October 2016 I helped install a climate station near Silverton, Colorado, to measure weather in the watershed after the 2015 Gold King Mine spill. Rider Resources worked with the [Mountain Studies Institute](https://www.mountainstudies.org/waterandsnowwork/climate-station-monitoring) for the Colorado Division of Reclamation, Mining and Safety.

The station has two parts: an upper site high in Minnehaha Basin, with a tower and a precipitation gauge, and a lower site with a precipitation gauge near the water treatment plant. It sends its data by radio. My part was the installation.
